Build a single-page site for the spring volunteer drive of Don Banks Clean-Up, a small charity that clears litter and Himalayan balsam from the banks of the River Don in Sheffield. It needs 300 volunteers for twelve Saturday sessions from March to June; each session runs from 10 am to 1 pm, and gloves, litter pickers and waders are supplied. Last year volunteers lifted 4.2 tonnes of rubbish, including 61 shopping trolleys. Audience: local residents, students, and teams from local employers looking for a volunteering day. Primary action: sign up for a session. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one folder, no build step, no external images (you may use web fonts).
