Build a single-page site for Sutondo, a 34-cover Basque restaurant on Call Lane in Leeds. Pintxos are served at the bar from 5 pm; the dining room serves a set menu of grilled hake, turbot and aged beef chop cooked over an oak-fired grill, with cider poured from height at the table. It opens Wednesday to Sunday and takes walk-ins at the bar only. Audience: people in Leeds planning a dinner out, and visitors to the city. Primary action: book a table. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one folder, no build step, no external images (you may use web fonts).
